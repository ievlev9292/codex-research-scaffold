# Editorial decision examples

Read when the correct intervention is unclear. Examples are synthetic editorial fixtures, not factual claims or wording templates.

## Empty framing around a protected result

Before: "It is important to note that, at 2.0 K, the measured linewidth was 0.18 meV, underscoring the exciting nature of this investigation."

After: "At 2.0 K, the measured linewidth was 0.18 meV."

The deleted language supplies no independent scientific proposition. Both precision and units remain unchanged.

## A substantive interpretation stays

Before: "The linewidth narrows below 2.0 K, demonstrating that the transition is continuous."

After: unchanged, with a brief note: "The stated link between linewidth narrowing and a continuous transition needs scientific confirmation; I preserved it."

Do not turn "demonstrating" into "suggesting" as a style fix. That changes the claim's strength.

## Preserve uncertainty and technical spans

Before: "It is worth noting that the signal is consistent with $I(t)=I_0e^{-t/\tau}$ for $\tau=(3.2\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{ps}$~\cite{Lee2024}; this does not exclude a second channel."

After: "The signal is consistent with $I(t)=I_0e^{-t/\tau}$ for $\tau=(3.2\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{ps}$~\cite{Lee2024}; this does not exclude a second channel."

The equation, units, uncertainty, citation, and limitation are unchanged.

## Courtesy without a changed commitment

Before: "We are profoundly grateful to the reviewer for this exceptionally insightful suggestion. We have added the calibration procedure in Sec. 2, but we have not repeated the measurement."

After: "We have added the calibration procedure in Sec. 2, but we have not repeated the measurement."

Removing ceremony must not imply that the requested measurement was performed.

## A real contrast needs no repair

"The field changes the phase, not the amplitude." Keep it if it states the intended distinction. A simple contrast, technical repetition, or a useful three-item list is not inherently formulaic.

## Undefined coined terminology

"The inference-resilience envelope widens near threshold." Preserve and flag "inference-resilience envelope" as undefined in the supplied material. It may be a local definition or a real specialist term; do not substitute "confidence interval."

## A caption that is already sufficient

Title: "Linewidth near threshold"

Caption: "(a) Linewidth versus pump power. Dashed line: fit to Eq. (2). Error bars: one standard deviation."

Leave these unchanged when they match the figure. Do not invent axes, units, panel descriptions, or a takeaway.

## No authorized causal connection

Before: "The drift decreased after calibration. The cause is unresolved."

After: unchanged.

"Calibration reduced the drift" would add causation. Even a plausible inferred connection is new scientific content.
